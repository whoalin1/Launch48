Privacy

This is a short notice for a productized design/build service. It is written in plain language on purpose.

What we collect

When you send a brief we collect the fields you fill in: business name, pitch, audience, tone, reference URLs, colors, must-have sections, and your contact email. When payment is wired we also receive whatever the payment provider shares with us (typically name, email, and that a $349 payment succeeded).

What we use it for

To design and ship your landing page, to email you the live URL, to handle the one revision round, and to process a refund if we miss 48 hours. We do not sell this information.

This preview

The form on this static preview does not transmit your brief. Submitting it only shows a “preview mode — payments not wired” message in your browser. Nothing is stored on a server from that form.

How long we keep it

Briefs and project files are kept as long as needed to deliver the page and the revision, then for ordinary bookkeeping. You can email us and ask us to delete project files that are not legally required to retain.

Contact

Questions about this notice: use the email you would put on the brief, addressed to Launch48, launch48.xyz. This preview is not a live inbox.
